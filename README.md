# 🤝 CrisisConnect UK
### Free digital food voucher platform — get help in minutes, not days

## The Problem
Over 1 in 10 UK households face food insecurity. The current process
requires phone calls, referral codes and paper vouchers — too many
steps for vulnerable people in crisis.

## What This Does
1. Fill a 2-minute form
2. Get an instant digital voucher with QR code
3. Walk into any food bank and show it
4. Or request free home delivery

No phone calls. No referral codes. No waiting.

## Live Tool
👉 [crisisconnect-uk.streamlit.app](https://foodreach-uk-efgozizx97eqfmvonwqqwm.streamlit.app/))

## Who It Helps
- Elderly people who cannot travel
- People with disabilities
- Low income families
- Anyone in sudden food crisis
